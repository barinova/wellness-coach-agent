## Tonight: lemon–garlic chicken with rice and green beans

**One plate:** brown rice, pan-seared chicken thigh strips, steamed green beans, olive oil, lemon.

**Steps (~15 min)**
1. Start rice in a small pot (or use microwave pouch rice).
2. Trim beans; steam in a covered pan with a splash of water (~5–6 min).
3. Slice chicken; salt and pepper. Pan-fry in olive oil ~6–7 min until cooked through.
4. Off heat: squeeze lemon over chicken; add a little minced garlic if you like (no hot sauce).
5. Plate rice, chicken, beans; drizzle a little olive oil on the beans.

**Why it fits:** dairy-free, no nuts/seeds, no fish, mild flavor, one plate, vegetable-heavy.
